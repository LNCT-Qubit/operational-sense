# Success Metrics

Proposed measures to define and baseline during implementation:
- 100% of approved assessments have an asset ID, framework version, weights version, assessor, and date.
- 100% of scores are constrained to integers 1–5.
- Every score of 4 or 5 has a recorded rationale.
- Reproducible calculations match reference test cases.
- Retrieved recommendations provide traceable evidence and identify when evidence is insufficient.
- Data-quality failures and retrieval evaluation results are reported rather than hidden.
