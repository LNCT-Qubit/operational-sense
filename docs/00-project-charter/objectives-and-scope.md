# Objectives and Scope

## Initial scope
- Establish an industrial asset and equipment taxonomy.
- Define a transparent, repeatable asset-criticality method.
- Keep source provenance, assessment versions, rationales, and approval status.
- Provide foundations for backend APIs and evidence-aware retrieval.

## Out of scope for the current starter
- Production deployment or live plant integration.
- Treating illustrative scenarios as real site data.
- Replacing site risk assessments, safety studies, or regulatory classifications.
- Claiming that proposed weights and thresholds are industry standards.
