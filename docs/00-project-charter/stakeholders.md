# Stakeholders & Target Users

*Operational Sense — Team Qubit (EPAM × LNCT Center of Excellence Program)*

## Target Users and Stakeholder Matrix

| Role | Category | Relationship to the Problem & System Ownership |
|---|---|---|
| **Machine operators & frontline technicians** | Primary Users | Experience the problem daily — need faster, evidence-backed troubleshooting in <30s without fighting vocabulary mismatches. |
| **Maintenance / reliability engineers** | Core Beneficiaries & Reviewers | Own repeat-failure and RCA outcomes; need reusable diagnostic history, ISO 14224/FMEA taxonomy alignment, and verified evidence chains. |
| **Shift supervisors** | Governance & Approvers | Verify outcomes; accountable for downtime, escalation load, and authorization of Tier 3 intrusive troubleshooting interventions. |
| **Plant / operations managers** | Executive Buyers | Accountable for plant MTTR, downtime cost, safety compliance, and operational knowledge-continuity risk across shift changes and retirements. |
| **IT & security stakeholders** | Governance & Infrastructure | Own data integration (CMMS/ERP APIs), data residency, role-based access control (RBAC), multi-tenant isolation, and corporate AI-usage approval. |
