# Acceptance Criteria

- Invalid factor scores outside 1–5 are rejected.
- The weighted score and index follow the documented formula and rounding rule.
- Override rules are applied after band classification and can only raise the category.
- Exactly one assessment per asset may be marked current.
- Example data is labelled synthetic/illustrative.
- Criticality and failure-case severity are separate fields.
- Source references and review status are available to users of approved records.
