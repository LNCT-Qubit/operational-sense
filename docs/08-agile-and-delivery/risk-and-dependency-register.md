# Risk and Dependency Register

| Risk/dependency | Impact | Mitigation/status |
|---|---|---|
| Weights and thresholds are unapproved | Results may not fit site needs | Obtain cross-functional approval and calibrate |
| Draft equipment hierarchy may change | IDs/foreign keys may be invalid | Reconcile before import |
| Example data is illustrative | Misuse as real plant evidence | Label origin and prohibit operational use |
| Standards full text not reviewed | Mapping/claims may be incomplete | Verify editions and permitted access |
| Backend/AI review outstanding | Integration assumptions may be wrong | Conduct design review |
| Runtime scaffolds unconfigured | CI/deployment claims could be misleading | Configure and test before release |
