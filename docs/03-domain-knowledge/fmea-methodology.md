# FMEA and Criticality Methodology

Asset criticality estimates the consequence of losing an asset's function; it is not a failure probability, current condition score, or failure-case severity. Proposed factors and weights: Safety 30%, Production 25%, Environment 15%, Dependency 15%, Maintenance 15%.

Weighted score = S×0.30 + P×0.25 + E×0.15 + D×0.15 + M×0.15. Index = weighted score × 20, rounded to one decimal. Bands: Low 20.0–39.9; Medium 40.0–59.9; High 60.0–79.9; Critical 80.0–100.0. Override floors: safety ≥5 or environment ≥5 => Critical; safety ≥4 or environment ≥4 => at least High.

These weights, bands, time bands, and rules are proposals—not an industry standard—and need approval and site calibration. Score only the worst credible consequence under normal operation, considering only standby and workarounds that actually exist. Record rationales for scores 4 or 5.
