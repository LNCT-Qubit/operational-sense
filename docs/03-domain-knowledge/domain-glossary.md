# Domain Glossary

- **Asset instance:** A specific installed, tagged unit (for example, P-101).
- **Equipment type:** A category such as centrifugal pump.
- **Asset criticality:** Consequence-based rating for losing an asset's function.
- **Failure-case severity:** Seriousness of a specific failure case; separate from asset criticality.
- **Standby:** Installed backup equipment and its changeover capability.
- **Override rule:** Rule that raises the category floor when a safety or environmental score is high.
- **Synthetic scenario:** Illustrative example not collected from a real plant.
- **Provenance:** Record of where data or a claim came from and how it was processed.
