# Assumptions and Limitations

- Framework weights, bands, default time bands, and overrides are draft team proposals.
- Standards were identified from catalogue/abstract or contents previews; their full text was not reviewed.
- Practitioner and vendor sources were only partly read through search excerpts.
- The method is consequence-based and does not score failure likelihood, current condition, or failure history.
- Regulatory exposure is represented within safety/environment scales, not as a separate factor.
- Example service contexts are synthetic and must not be represented as site data.
- Reassess at least every 12 months as a proposed interval, and after process/standby changes, serious incidents, or changes to framework parameters.
- This framework does not replace site risk assessments, safety studies, or regulatory classifications.
