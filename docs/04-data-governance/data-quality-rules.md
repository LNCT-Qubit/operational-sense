# Data Quality Rules

- IDs are unique and follow approved formats.
- Foreign keys resolve to known assets, types, classes, cases, and sources.
- Criticality scores are integers from 1 to 5.
- Rationales are present for scores 4 or 5.
- Derived values match the versioned formula.
- Exactly one current assessment exists per asset.
- Origin and approval status are explicit.
- Dates, enums, units, and source provenance are validated.
- Synthetic examples are never mislabeled as site assessments.
